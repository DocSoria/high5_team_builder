Step 1

Choose your team challenge

Imagine you're coordinating a University of Idaho project. You can recruit three fictional colleagues. Every candidate has five HIGH5 strengths. Your job is not to find the “perfect” combination; it's to consider the contributions people might make and the conversations a team needs to have.

Important: Strengths offer possible starting points for conversation, not proof of capability or suitability. Experiences, skills, interests, work capacity, and consent matter too.
